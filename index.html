Whitenoise

Free white, pink, brown, grey, green and blue noise, plus calming rain, stream, ocean and breeze sounds. Every sound is generated live in your browser, so it never loops, and there are no ads or sign-ups.

Noise colors

  • Blue noise: tilted toward the highs; a bright, airy hiss for focus.
  • White noise: equal power at every frequency; best at masking speech.
  • Grey noise: shaped so every pitch sounds equally loud.
  • Pink noise: softer highs, like steady rainfall.
  • Green noise: centred on the midrange, like the hum of the outdoors.
  • Brown noise: deep and rounded, like a distant waterfall.
  • Deep brown noise: a low, heavy rumble.

Nature sounds

Soft rain, a gentle stream, slow ocean waves, a light breeze, the muffled calm of being underwater, a crackling campfire and crickets on a summer night.

Timers

A sleep timer that fades out gently instead of stopping abruptly, and a focus timer that lowers the sound during breaks.
